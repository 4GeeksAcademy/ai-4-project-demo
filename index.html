ABOUT ME

I'm Marlowe Reeves — a mixed-media artist working across collage, photography, and installation. My work lives at the intersection of the discarded and the deliberate, finding beauty in misregistration, offset inks, and the ghostly halftones of cheap reproduction.

Born in Portland, OR (1992). Currently based in Brooklyn, NY with a studio in Bushwick. Graduate of the Rhode Island School of Design (BFA 2015).

— everything absorbs something —

CAREER / WORKS

2025 — “GHOST HALFTONE”

Solo exhibition at The Bunker Gallery, Brooklyn. A series of large-scale collages printed on newsprint and overlaid with screen-printed halftone dots. Reviewed in Artforum.

2023 — RESIDENCY @ SFA

Three-month residency at the San Francisco Art Institute. Produced a site-specific installation using reclaimed film posters and misprinted zines from the 1990s.

2021 — “FEEDBACK LOOP”

Two-person show with J. D. Woo at Public Fiction, Los Angeles. Explored the visual noise of photocopy generation — images of images of images.

2019 — PUBLIC ART COMMISSION

Permanent collage mural for the Portland Central Library — 12 panels made from deconstructed book jackets and library due-date slips.
